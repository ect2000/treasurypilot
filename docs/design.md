# Design direction

Visual thesis: a charcoal treasury workspace with paper-white data, precise orange signals and the quiet density of an institutional control room.

Content plan: a full-canvas TreasuryPilot poster with a living liquidity path; a short explanation of interpretation, deterministic protection and execution; the working treasury workspace; one clear invitation to open the scenario.

Interaction thesis: cash pulses along an SVG path at entry, decisions move between states during replanning, and a restrained inspector slides in to explain policy and authorize an exact action. All honor reduced motion.

The dashboard is a workspace, not a card mosaic: compact navigation, one KPI band, a liquidity chart, an obligation ledger and a contextual inspector. Live balances and the synthetic operating allocation are always named separately.
