## Starter Kit 1 fit

The product reads balances, considers multiple obligations and currencies, forecasts liquidity, protects a reserve, changes autonomy with evidence confidence and prepares a concrete action. The differentiator is a changing plan with inspectable reasons and exact approval binding, demonstrated against real Sandbox APIs.

No additional Starter Kit 1 enablement was needed: the verified account already had wallet reads, FX rates/quotes/conversions, existing beneficiaries, transfers and simulation permissions. The active Netherlands EUR Global Account already exists. No new Global Account or beneficiary was created.
